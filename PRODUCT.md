# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

DeFi protocol contributors, delegates, donors, researchers, and community members
who want to understand the Collective's purpose, operating model, financial
position, and recent work.

## Product Purpose

The site is the public record and primary reference point for the DeFi
Collective. It should make the organization, its operating mode, its policies,
its reports, and ways to support it easy to find and verify. Success means that
readers can quickly reach accurate current information without losing access to
important historical context.

## Brand Personality

Collective, transparent, and pragmatic. The site should feel direct and
community-operated rather than promotional, while retaining the established ant
motif and recognizable visual identity.

## Anti-references

Do not turn the site into a polished SaaS marketing template, a trend-led crypto
landing page, or a dense institutional portal. Avoid a broad visual redesign,
decorative feature expansion, and modernization work that does not improve
content access, credibility, or maintainability.

## Design Principles

1. Put current, decision-relevant information first.
2. Preserve the public record when content is consolidated or retired.
3. Prefer clear evidence and direct language over promotional framing.
4. Keep the established identity while removing avoidable friction and weight.
5. Make every core reading and navigation path work on small screens and without
   a pointer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG 2.2 AA as the working standard. Core content and navigation must support
keyboard use, visible focus, readable contrast, semantic headings, meaningful
link names, reduced motion preferences, and mobile viewports down to 320 px.
